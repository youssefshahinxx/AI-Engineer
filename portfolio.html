About me

I'm building toward the intersection of AI and business.

My background started with web development and backend systems. Over time, I became increasingly interested in AI — not only using AI tools, but understanding how AI agents can become part of larger systems and actually perform useful work.

Today, my interests sit around AI agents, workflow automation, APIs, backend systems, databases, conversational AI, and business-process automation.

I enjoy working between the business and technical sides: understanding a process, identifying what should be automated, deciding where AI is genuinely useful, and then building the system that connects everything together.

A note on my experience

I'm deliberately honest about where I am. I have built and experimented with AI and automation systems, but I have not yet operated a large enterprise automation environment with real business data. I'm looking for opportunities where I can take the technical foundation I've built and apply it to real production problems.

Automation mindset

AI should solve the process, not just generate the response.

When I design an automation, I think about the entire lifecycle: trigger, data, decision, action, human handoff, failure handling and measurement.
